### Tools

- **[lifekeeper](https://github.com/nubifere/lifekeeper)**: reclaim your data and start your digital self, with everything about you in one place on your own computer. [Download](https://github.com/nubifere/lifekeeper/releases/latest)
- **[peekdata](https://github.com/nubifere/peekdata)**: open data files in your browser to browse, filter, profile, query and chart them, or ask questions in plain language
- **[ask-your-db](https://github.com/nubifere/ask-your-db)**: upload documents, spreadsheets, slides or emails and ask questions about them, with sources. [Try it](https://ask-your-db.streamlit.app)

### Projects

- **[Moon Base Fire Evidence](https://github.com/claudiakitova/moon-base-fire-evidence)**: an open dataset and dashboard of NASA microgravity combustion tests, for NASA Space Apps 2026. [Dashboard](https://kitova.me/moon-fire/)
- **[physics-talks](https://github.com/claudiakitova/physics-talks)**: a chatbot that answers condensed matter physics questions from a library of scientific papers. [Try it](https://physics-talks.streamlit.app)
- **[data-stories](https://github.com/claudiakitova/data-stories)**: real-life questions answered with data, from crypto forecasts and app reviews to life support in space

Issues and pull requests are welcome in any of them.
